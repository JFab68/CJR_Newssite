Justice Now AZ collects headlines from public news feeds — local Arizona outlets, national criminal justice newsrooms, and targeted news searches.

Each story is sorted into a **level** (Local, Regional, National) and one or more **sections** (Policing; Prosecution & Courts; Jails & Prisons; Probation & Parole; Post-conviction & Reentry; Oversight & Transparency) based on its source and its wording.

Stories that mention themes such as oversight, accountability, transparency, deaths in custody, misconduct, audits, lawsuits, abuse, investigations, or ombudsman work are weighted higher and may be featured in **Oversight Watch**.

Every headline links to the original publisher. We never republish full articles.
